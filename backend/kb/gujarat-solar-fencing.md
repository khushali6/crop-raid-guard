# Gujarat solar fencing assistance

## Purpose

Gujarat provides financial assistance to farmers for installing solar-powered fencing around their fields to protect crops from wild animals. A solar fence gives a short, safe electric pulse that discourages animals such as nilgai and wild boar from entering the field.

## Known gap

The scheme summary notes that a weakness of the scheme is low awareness: many eligible farmers do not know that the assistance exists. Farmers in forest-edge villages who face repeated crop raids should ask their agriculture office about it.

## How to apply

Check the scheme page on myScheme for current eligibility, benefit amount and documents, and apply through the official state portal or the agriculture office. Do not buy or install a fence before confirming whether prior approval is required for the assistance.

## Safety

Only use energisers designed for agricultural fencing. Never connect a fence directly to mains electricity; this is dangerous to people and animals and is illegal.
