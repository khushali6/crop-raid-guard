# Keralam wildlife compensation increase

## Announcement

On 29 September 2026, the Keralam (Kerala) government announced that it will double the compensation paid for deaths caused by wildlife attacks and for crop losses caused by wild animals.

## What farmers should do

Compensation for crop loss from wild animals in Kerala is handled through the state forest department. Farmers should apply to the local forest office with evidence of the damage, such as photographs, the date and time of the incident and any camera footage. Rates and procedures can change; confirm the current amount and documents at the forest range office.

## Note on other states

Compensation rules differ by state and are revised from time to time. Information about Kerala does not apply to Gujarat, Maharashtra or other states.
