# Common crop-raiding animals

## Nilgai (blue bull)

Large antelope found across north and western India including Gujarat. Feeds on wheat, gram, mustard, vegetables and other standing crops, often at dusk, night and early morning, and frequently in groups. Can jump low fences.

## Wild boar

Mostly active at night. Digs up and tramples crops such as maize, groundnut, potato, sugarcane and paddy, and can dig under fences. Often visits the same field repeatedly over several nights.

## Indian peafowl

The national bird, protected under the Wildlife (Protection) Act. Feeds on seeds, seedlings, groundnut and vegetables during the day. Use only gentle, non-harmful deterrents.

## Rhesus macaque and langur

Active during the day. Damage fruits, vegetables, maize and groundnut. Usually come in troops.

## Jackal

Mostly active at night. Can damage maize, groundnut, sugarcane and melons.

## Chital and other deer

Graze on young crops near forest edges, mostly at dawn, dusk and night.

## Elephant

Causes heavy damage in forest-edge areas of some states. Do not approach; inform the forest department immediately.
