# Low-cost crop protection methods

These are commonly used, low-cost methods. Results vary by animal, crop and place; ask your local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) or agriculture office what works in your area.

## Watch and ward

Night watch from a raised platform (machan) during the weeks when the crop is most attractive, such as maize cobbing or groundnut pod filling. Use camera alerts to know the hours when animals usually arrive and focus watch at those times.

## Barriers

Wire fencing, trenches and live hedges of thorny plants can slow animals down. Wild boar often dig under fences, so bury the lower edge of the wire or add a low strand close to the ground. Gujarat offers assistance for wire fencing and solar fencing; check the scheme documents.

## Deterrents

Chilli-smoke (burning dried chilli with dung cakes on the windward side), chilli-oil rope fences, bright lights, noise makers and moving flags are used by farmers against many crop raiders. Animals get used to the same deterrent over time, so change methods and positions regularly.

## Crop planning

Where possible, avoid planting the most attractive crops next to forest edges, and harvest promptly when the crop is ready.

## What not to do

Do not use poison, snares, explosives or live electric wires connected to mains power. These are illegal under wildlife and electricity laws and are dangerous to people, livestock and protected animals.
