# Gujarat wire fencing assistance

## Purpose

The Gujarat government provides assistance to farmers for putting up wire fencing around their farm to protect crops from damage by wild and stray animals.

## Amount of assistance

The assistance for wire fencing is ₹200 per running metre or 50% of the cost, whichever is lower.

## How to apply

Applications for Gujarat agriculture assistance schemes are generally made through the state's farmer portal or the local agriculture office. Farmers should confirm the current application window, eligibility (such as minimum land holding or group applications) and the documents required with the taluka or district agriculture office before buying material, because assistance usually requires prior approval.

## Tips

- Keep photographs and records of the animal visits that caused crop loss; they support the case for fencing in the worst-affected fields first.
- Combine fencing with other measures such as watch and ward at night, because animals such as wild boar can dig under fences.
