# PMFBY wild animal attack add-on

## What changed

From the Kharif 2026 season, crop loss caused by wild animal attacks is covered under the Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY), the national crop insurance scheme. Wild animal attack is added as the fifth add-on cover under localised risks, alongside the other localised risk covers. Paddy inundation was brought under the scheme in the same revision.

## Which animals and districts are covered

States notify which wild animals are covered and which districts are considered vulnerable. Cover therefore depends on your state's notification. Farmers should check with their state agriculture department, bank or insurance company whether their district and the animal that damaged their crop are notified.

## How to report a loss

Farmers must report the crop loss within 72 hours of the incident. The report is made through the Crop Insurance App, with geotagged photographs of the damaged crop. The helpline of the insurance company can also be used. The official report must be made by the farmer through these official channels; a private app or evidence pack does not replace the official intimation.

## Verification

News reports state that losses may be verified using drones before compensation is paid. Keeping clear, timestamped evidence of the animal visit and the damage helps the farmer answer questions during verification.

## Practical checklist

- Report within 72 hours of the incident, through the Crop Insurance App or helpline.
- Take geotagged photographs of the damaged area.
- Keep the camera footage and any evidence pack that shows the date, time and animal.
- Note your policy number, survey number and the affected area; these are needed in the report.
