# PMFBY revision: wild animal attacks

## Summary of the ministry statement

The Union agriculture ministry announced that crop losses due to wild animal attacks will be covered under the Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana from the Kharif 2026 season. The cover is provided as an add-on under localised risks. State governments decide which animals are covered and identify the vulnerable districts where the cover applies.

## Reporting rule

Affected farmers must report the loss within 72 hours through the Crop Insurance App, and upload geotagged photographs as evidence of the damage.

## What this means for a farmer

Because the claim depends on a report within 72 hours with photographic evidence, a farmer who records animal visits on a camera and keeps timestamped clips is better prepared to file on time. The farmer should still confirm with the bank, insurer or agriculture office that the crop, district and animal are notified for the season.
